Put foner on your iPhone home screen
It takes about two minutes, and then foner opens like any other app.
1
 Open Safari on your iPhone and go to app.foner.uk.
It needs to be Safari, the browser with the blue compass icon.
2
Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
Can't see it? Tap the three dots (•••) first, then tap Share.
3
Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
If it isn't in the list, tap View More or Edit Actions at the bottom to find it.
4
 Tap Add in the top right corner.
If you see a switch called Open as Web App, leave it turned on.
5
Find the new foner icon on your home screen, open it, and sign in with the email and temporary password above.
The home screen app keeps its own login, so sign in here even if you already did in Safari. You'll stay signed in after that.
6
 Change your password. Tap Account at the bottom right, type a new password under Change password, and tap Update password.
What you'll find inside
Home shows the people waiting for a call back. Tap Call or WhatsApp, then the tick once you've dealt with them.
Calls has every call with the caller's details, a short summary, the recording and the full conversation. You can add your own notes too.
Receptionist shows what it tells callers. Tap Change to update your areas or services, or tell us how calls should be handled. You'll see when it's done.
